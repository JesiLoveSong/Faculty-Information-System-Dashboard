# Faculty-Information-System-Engagement

**Project Overview**
---

This project examined how faculty and administrators engaged with the university's Faculty Information System (Interfolio) by analyzing user adoption, evaluation activity, faculty reporting behaviors, and workflow performance. I collected and analyzed system usage data, developed a series of interactive dashboards in Google Data Studio, and translated complex operational metrics into actionable insights for senior leadership to support institutional decision-making, system planning, and process improvement

**Question:**
---

How do faculty engage the faculty information system?

**Project Objectives**
---

- Understand how faculty and administrators engage with the Faculty Information System.
- Provide leadership with a centralized view of system adoption, evaluation activity, and workflow performance.
- Identify college-level trends and variations in faculty reporting and evaluation processes.
- Monitor operational effectiveness and opportunities for process improvement.
- Inform future decisions regarding system implementation, training strategies, and faculty information system planning.

**Data Sources**
---

- Faculty Information System (FIS) Activity Input Reports
- FIS Log in metrics
- FIS email accont
- FIS case workflow reports

**Methodology**
---

**Data Collection**

- Extracted user activity, workflow, evaluation, and Faculty Activity Reporting (FAR) data from Interfolio.
- Consolidated and validated system records using Excel.
- Reviewed historical usage trends and faculty engagement metrics across academic colleges.
- Standardized datasets to support consistent reporting and cross-college comparisons

**Data Analysis**

- Conducted user behavior analysis to evaluate system adoption and engagement.
- Measured evaluation activity and case volume across colleges.
- Analyzed Faculty Activity Reporting submissions by activity category.
- Assessed workflow performance through case creation, progression, and support metrics.
- Evaluated training outcomes through changes in college-level system independence and usage patterns

**Reporting and Visualization**

- Developed interactive dashboards in Google Data Studio.
- Designed executive-level quarterly report highlighting user adoption, evaluation activity, workflow efficiency, and faculty reporting trends.
- Created drill-down functionality to support institution-wide and college-level analysis

**Tools Used**
---

- Excel
- FIS
- Google Data Studio
- Canva

**Key Findings**
---

- The FIS Usage Dashboard provided a consolidated view of system adoption, evaluation activity, faculty activity reporting, and case distribution across colleges. The dashboard showed 6,289 total users, including 2,106 faculty users, alongside 2,893 evaluation cases for the 2024–2025 academic year and 576 award cases for the 2025–2026 academic year.
- Faculty Activity Reporting data showed 1,913 faculty workload inputted, with instructional activities representing the largest share of reported activity (47.7%), followed by research and creative activities (22.6%).
- The dashboard also provided visibility into post-tenure review (PTR) and promotion and tenure (P&T) activity by college, helping identify where evaluation processes were most concentrated.
- The quarterly system activity dashboard added insight into system engagement and operational performance.
- The FIS recorded 948 logins and 2,188 cases created during the quarter, with an average of 87% of cases progressing on or ahead of schedule. The team submitted 19 support requests. 

**Business Recommendations**
---

- Continue training and decentralization efforts that allow colleges to manage committees, create cases, and advance workflows independently, reducing reliance on central administrative support.
- Regularly review dashboard metrics to identify colleges with lower engagement levels and provide targeted outreach, training, or support where needed.
- Leverage Faculty Activity Reporting data to better understand how faculty effort is distributed across teaching, research, service, and administrative responsibilities.
- Incorporate workflow completion rates, case progression metrics, and support request trends into routine administrative reporting to proactively identify process bottlenecks.
- Use engagement and utilization data to inform future decisions regarding permanent Faculty Information System selection, implementation, and expansion strategies.
  
**Analytical Limitations**
---

While the dashboard provided a comprehensive view of FIS system activity, several limitations should be considered when interpreting results.

- System usage metrics measured engagement within the FIS but could not fully capture user satisfaction, workflow quality, or effectiveness of individual processes.
- Differences in evaluation case volumes may reflect variations in college size, organizational structure, faculty composition, or review requirements rather than differences in system adoption alone.
- Faculty Activity Reporting data reflected submitted activities and may not fully capture all faculty work occurring outside the reporting framework.
- Login activity and case creation metrics provide indicators of engagement but cannot independently explain how effectively users completed tasks or utilized system features.

**Conclusion**
---

The FIS Engagement Dashboard transformed complex system data into an accessible reporting environment that supported institutional oversight, operational management, and strategic planning. By consolidating user adoption, evaluation activity, faculty reporting, workflow performance, and support metrics into a single dashboard, the project provided leadership with a comprehensive view of how the Faculty Information System was being utilized across the university.

The analysis demonstrated strong system adoption, increasing college-level independence, and effective workflow performance while identifying opportunities for continued monitoring and process improvement. Ultimately, the project highlighted how business intelligence reporting can convert operational system data into actionable insights that support informed decision-making and long-term institutional planning.
